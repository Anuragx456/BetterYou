## Full Description

Transform your nutrition and gym performance with BetterYou — your offline-first wellness copilot combining instant AI plate computer vision, supermarket ingredient risk screening, and progressive strength tracking.

No cloud account required. 100% private, local-first data ownership.

### 🥗 INSTANT PLATE AI VISION & MACRO TRACKING
• Snap a photo of any plate or homemade Indian dish (Paneer Butter Masala, Biryani, Dosa, Dal) to estimate calories and macronutrients in under 2 seconds.
• Interactive portion multipliers (0.5x, 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2.0x) automatically recalculate macros in real time.
• Built-in thermodynamic plausibility validation checks estimates to prevent unrealistic calculations.
• Offline database lookup for staple Indian foods with voice dictation and manual override.

### 🔬 INGREDIENT RISK SCREENING LAB
• Scan barcodes or snap back-of-pack ingredient labels to inspect food processing and chemical additives.
• Context-aware Indian market screening: Flags common packaging traps including 80% mineral oil hair formulations, synthetic pyrethroids in mosquito vaporizers, SLS oral irritants, and hidden sugars.
• Deterministic 0–100 Safety Scores grounded in FSSAI, CDSCO, BIS, and EFSA regulatory monographs.
• Instant offline preset lookups for staple Indian grocery products without internet access.

### 🏋️‍♂️ STRENGTH TRACKING & PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD
• Built for serious lifters: Log exercises, sets, weights (kg), and reps with zero lag on the gym floor.
• Pre-built routines (Push, Pull, Leg) or create unlimited custom workouts.
• Floating auto-rest timer (60s, 90s, 120s) with background notifications so you never miss your next set.
• Automatic Personal Record (PR) detection for weight, reps, and total workout volume.
• Epley formula estimated 1RM progression charts for compound lifts.

### 🔒 LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY & DATA OWNERSHIP
• Embedded SQLite database stores all meals, workouts, exercises, and scanned products on your device.
• Sandboxed media storage: Photos stay on your phone, never uploaded to a cloud database.
• Full JSON and CSV export/import: Backup your entire history and restore it whenever you switch phones.
• Zero third-party ad SDKs, tracking pixels, or data brokers.
• Optional BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) mode: Connect directly to Google AI Studio using your own personal Gemini API key stored in encrypted hardware keystores.
